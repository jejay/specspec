# Change selected binding passages without resending the whole body

People and coding agents can revise selected literal passages of an existing
binding while retaining unrelated body text and node metadata. Small edits remain
concise and reviewable in ordinary command arguments, individually or alongside
other coherent editing steps. Uncertain or stale text matches remain explicit
rather than selecting an unintended passage.
