# Make graph colors convey selectable meaning

Use colors more effectively. In the total graph, users can choose coloring by
distance to the manifesto or another understandable, non-approval property.
Keep the meaning of fills understandable alongside Git changes and withdrawal
history. Do not color nodes as proposed/accepted, or encode a review-confidence
score into their metadata or appearance.

Origin: the user's graph-color request and subsequent removal of proposed/accepted
workflow. No palette or alternative-property implementation is prescribed here.
