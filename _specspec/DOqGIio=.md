# Understand command use directly from help

Owners can understand each command and option without already knowing its internals
or having to find an external guide. Help explains what an operation is for, what
its inputs refer to, how defaults behave and what effects to expect. Complete
examples and safety explanations support ordinary human use as well as agents.
