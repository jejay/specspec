# Align node colors along a soft rainbow and offer revision-status fills

Distance spectrum modes use an ordered rainbow starting at violet and ending
at red, repeating when necessary. Keep the current gently colored feel while
adding intermediate colors and realigning hues; non-spectrum node-type colors
choose appropriate entries from the same rainbow table.

Add a "Git/Revision Status" color scheme. Choose suitable rainbow entries close
to conventional added green, modified blue and deleted red (and distinguish other
comparison cues appropriately). This colors nodes from the selected comparison,
not approval/review status. Unlike distance/type schemes, it can color the manifesto
by its revision status too. Distance/type modes keep the manifesto light gray.

Origin: the user's rainbow-table and Git/Revision Status request. Exact swatches,
cycle indexing and neutral/rename/source-only/draft choices remain conclusions.
