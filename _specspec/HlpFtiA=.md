# Keep linear and logarithmic influence choices together

Offer Absolute influence, Relative influence, Log Absolute Influence and Log
Relative Influence as distinct persistent Color by choices. Keep linear modes and
their logarithmic counterparts available for direct comparison in both hosts.

Logarithmic modes apply log(influence) to self-inclusive influence before
normalization; leaves have log(1) = 0. Linear modes transform influence by
subtracting one. Use the same absolute reference domain or maximum-manifesto-
distance cohorts, continuous red/yellow/green palette and independent Git/highlight
cues in each counterpart.
