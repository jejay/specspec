# Keep linear and logarithmic influence choices together

Offer Absolute influence, Relative influence, Log Absolute Influence and Log
Relative Influence as distinct persistent Color by choices. Keep the linear modes
and their logarithmic counterparts available for direct comparison in both hosts.

Logarithmic modes apply log(influence + 1) to raw unique descendant counts before
normalization. The +1 gives zero-descendant nodes a defined logarithm and permits
a self-inclusive interpretation of the transformed count. The influence count
itself remains the unique descendants excluding the node. Use the same absolute
reference domain or maximum-manifesto-distance cohorts as the linear counterpart,
and the same continuous red/yellow/green palette and independent Git/highlight cues.
