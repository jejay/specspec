# Preview personal-file conversion before replacing work

A version-one file from the personal planner can be imported: the app shows a preview of
the workshop plan it would become and explains which dates and priorities are lost and
that dates turn into weekday forecasts. The plan is replaced when the person presses
Apply; previewing or cancelling leaves the saved work and the browser storage as they
are.
