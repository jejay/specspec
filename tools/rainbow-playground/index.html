Suggested type / revision choices

Hues sweep linearly from start to end, not via the shortest rotation. Chroma is a useful saturation control, not HSL saturation. Out-of-gamut samples reduce chroma at fixed hue/lightness; reductions are reported. Hover paint may also be gamut-mapped by your browser. Perception varies by display.
