```text
# Host a hidden-role party game for one room

Six to ten friends in one room play on their own phones. Each player gets a secret role, and nobody learns who is on which side before the final reveal.
```

The owner writes:

Every New Year we play a game like this in a cabin, with paper slips and a lot of arguing about the rules. I want an app for it. What I have in mind:

- Players join with a four-letter room code, without an account.
- A timeline on the cabin's TV shows who did what and when, so everyone can follow the game.
- Each night phase lasts 90 seconds, so the evening does not drag.
- It has to work without internet, because the cabin has no reception. The host's phone is the server.
- It should be written in Kotlin.
- At the final reveal, everyone sees the full history of the game.
