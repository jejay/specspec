# Let the owner choose what the colors of the nodes mean

The Color by menu offers eight meanings for the fill of the nodes, each with an informative legend: Min. Distance to Manifesto, Max. Distance to Manifesto, Absolute influence, Relative influence, Log Absolute Influence, Log Relative Influence, Node type and Git/Revision Status. The distance modes show the shortest and the longest directed path from the manifesto. Node type shows the three types of node, and Git/Revision Status shows the selected comparison. The influence modes show influence, which includes the node itself, on a linear and on a logarithmic scale.

The fills that carry meaning, the Git colors of titles and letters, the line patterns of relationships and the marks for sources can each be understood on its own. Distances and influence are derived for each revision separately, and the chosen color meaning is kept as presentation state. The legend pairs the actual colors with short labels and with the ends of scales, and the tutorial explains paths and scaling. Cards show their downstream counts and Git marks in every mode.
