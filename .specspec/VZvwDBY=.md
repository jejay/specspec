# Keep every conclusion revisable beneath the intentions it was drawn from

Every node except the manifesto is a conclusion drawn from its parents, which are its intentions, and is in turn an intention for the nodes beneath it. Intention and conclusion name the two ends of a link; a node's place between the manifesto and the files follows from its links alone.

A conclusion is one valid conclusion. It rests on reasoning, stated or implied, and another can be drawn in its place at any time and at every depth. Each conclusion has a separately addressable place in the graph and explains how it serves its parents. Replacing a node leaves the nodes above it in force and puts the nodes beneath it up for review, so a changed manifesto can change the whole product.

The graph carries no measure of certainty. How firmly a statement holds is in the owner's head; it tends to be firmer near the manifesto, where more depends on it, and an owner can still care about a detail far down. The agent tells the owner's intentions, the conclusions the owner has drawn and its own conclusions apart through their relationships and reasoning.
