# Make every proposed change the best graph the agent can conceive

Whatever the agent proposes or carries out follows the rules for structure and wording in full and aims at the soundest graph it can conceive: in a structure review, in planning, and where a request reaches into sealed nodes. That a proposal is conceptually sound weighs more than how far it reaches; the reach is the agent's judgment.
