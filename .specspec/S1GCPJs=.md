# Keep the manifesto to intentions

The manifesto holds intentions only. It is the top of the graph, so it is the one node that the agent does not hold against an intention. While the manifesto is open, the agent may point out a conclusion in its wording that is not a given, such as a layout or a technology the owner has no reason to fix, and suggests a node beneath the manifesto for it. The owner changes the manifesto when their intentions change, and the agent then looks at the whole graph again, because every node depends on it.
