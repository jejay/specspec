# Decide depth and breadth by which statement depends on which

A link says that the child is in the graph because of its parent. The parent binds
whoever works on the child, and replacing the parent puts the child up for review. A
node is one conclusion: what is reviewed and replaced as a whole.

Two statements relate in one of four ways. One goes below the other when it loses its
reason without the other, while the other stands as written when the first is
replaced. They are siblings when each serves their parent on its own. They are one
node when they would be reviewed and replaced together. A statement names two parents
when it has to be reconsidered whenever either of them changes and neither descends
from the other.

A statement goes under the most specific node that is its reason, and no lower: a need
noticed while detailing a conclusion goes where it survives that conclusion.
