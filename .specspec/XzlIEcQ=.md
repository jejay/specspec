# Move smoothly over the graph between its relationships and readable detail

The owner moves smoothly over the graph, from the relationships of many nodes to the readable detail of one. From any position the owner finds the way back and reaches content that the view clips. Moving around works alongside the selection of nodes, editing in the form, unsaved drafts, comparisons of revisions and the context of a review.

Headlines are drawn clearly at high magnification, with the same logical font sizes and the same layout of the world as at actual size. Text selection, scrolling and the text cursor behave as usual while the view moves over the graph.
