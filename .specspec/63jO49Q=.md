# Resolve the members of a spotlight through its comparison and their node IDs

The status of each member and its current title and path are derived from the comparison of the spotlight: the revisions that it pinned, or the live view of the working files against HEAD. A selected full ID is looked up in the target first and then among the nodes that the base holds and the target deleted. Each member is reported as added, deleted, changed, unchanged, absent or unavailable.

A warning about drift of the working files describes that the context of the capture changed, and the highlight rings and the framing by node ID go on under it. A historical object that is missing and a graph in an unsupported format produce a clear diagnostic about the context. The committed content comes from Git, and the local spotlight data is metadata alone. The fields of the node files and the safeguards of editing work the same with and without spotlights.
