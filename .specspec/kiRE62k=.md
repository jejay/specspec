# Change selected passages of a node's text and keep the rest

People and coding agents change selected literal passages of an existing node's text, and the rest of the body and the metadata of the node stay as they are. A small edit is short to write and easy to review in ordinary command arguments, alone or together with the other steps of one coherent change. A passage that cannot be matched with certainty, or that the current text lacks because the text has changed, is reported, and no other passage is taken in its place.
