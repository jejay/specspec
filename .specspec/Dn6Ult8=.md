# Distinguish node types with square, rounder and circular outlines

Implementation cards have the sharpest outline, Requirement cards are rounder,
and the manifesto is a true circle in compact and expanded views. The selected palette and role mappings, native editing and readable content hold here too. Shape
presents the node grammar in the shared renderer.
