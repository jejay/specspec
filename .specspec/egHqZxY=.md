# Let the owner choose and change their depth in every region of the graph

The owner decides, region by region, how deep they go in the graph: how far they
write it themselves, and how far they read and understand what the agent drew. Every
depth is a workable place to stop, from the manifesto alone to the linked code,
because nodes run from intention to conclusion. Beyond the owner's depth the agent
concludes on its own and records its conclusions as nodes.

The owner moves this boundary throughout a project. A region skimmed at first can be
read closely and reworked later, while other regions keep a shallow reading. Hands-on
engineering and delegated work are the two ends of this range, and each region of one
project sits at its own point of it.
