# Keep the guide as short as the quizzes allow

A sentence stays in the guide when the quizzes show a loss without it. Wording is
trimmed by removing sentences or words from a guide version and repeating the quizzes
on it. A cut that leaves the results where they were is a candidate for the shipped
guide, and the owner decides which candidates it takes over.
