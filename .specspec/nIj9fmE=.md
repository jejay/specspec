# Preserve consent, physical safety and uncertainty through editing

The compact interface has exact selected-file deletion, uncommitted selected-
file warnings, whole-action consent, reviewed plan rechecking, original-byte checks,
null journals and recovery. Unknown started-mutation outcomes require rereading and
recovery rather than destructive replay. Graph-invalid results are reported rather
than mistaken for an unknown transaction outcome.

Strict/expanding source contracts, immutable blob anchors, exact-index
checks, explicit unavailable/ambiguous findings, literal previews and deliberate capture/reanchor hold here too. Project trust, native dirty guards and absent-buffer non-resurrection are independent of graph validity. Revision metrics describe their own known
relationships; historical context is distinct from the current node text. UI forms,
drafts/cameras, deadlines and default-off polling have their owners.
