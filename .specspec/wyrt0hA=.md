# Give the structure review a phase section in the guide

The guide's phases include the structure review between owner planning and tandem
planning. Its section says what starts it, names what the owner's edits are held
against, tells the agent to suggest and to leave the graph as it is, and says that an
accepted suggestion is applied in agent planning after the owner's commit, while
everything else stays in the owner's words. The section on agent planning names the
accepted suggestions among the things the agent works from.
