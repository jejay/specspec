# Hold every conclusion against its intentions, at any depth

The agent questions conclusions, and every node except the manifesto is a conclusion. It asks whether a statement makes sense for the intentions above it and whether another conclusion would serve them better, and it tells the owner. Statements far from the manifesto meet this question most often, because more of them is conclusion than intention. A statement that would sit directly beneath the manifesto meets it too: when it makes no sense for the product the manifesto describes, or works against it, the agent raises that. An example is a screen that shows every player's moves in a game whose manifesto keeps the players' roles secret.
