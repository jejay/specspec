# Show every relationship as an arrow whose direction is easy to read

The owner sees every relationship as a smooth arrow from the parent to the child. The arrow begins at the outline of the parent card and ends at the outline of the child card, its direction is clear whatever the distance between the two, and it runs around the content of the cards on its way. Lines and arrowheads are clearly visible when the graph is zoomed out, and the line of an arrow joins its filled arrowhead in a smooth tangent.

Hovering a card emphasizes the arrows that are drawn between it and its direct parents and children, each as a pair of curve and arrowhead. This works in the overview and in the view of a selected node, also for an expanded card and for the links of an earlier revision and of a draft. When the hover ends, the arrows return to their ordinary paint. An emphasized colored arrow gains brightness and saturation in its own hue. The line patterns that mark Git changes and the direction of every arrow are readable during the hover.
