# Count an answer only for the wording of the brief it was given

Every stored answer to an open task carries a mark of the brief's wording. When a brief is reworded, the answers to its earlier wording stop counting, and the task is asked afresh under its name. A dilemma is known by its ID, and a reworded dilemma takes a new ID.
