# Deepen realization and communicate contradictions during implementation

An implementing agent keeps the specification and the product in sync: it records what the graph leaves to it as conclusions, explains what it built in implementation leaves and keeps existing explanations and source links current. Removal decisions return to the owner. When a contradiction or potential product improvement appears, implement
a simple broad contradiction-free subset consistent with the owner's understanding,
and report the conflict, subset decisions and consequences. A proposal branch can
carry a separately reviewable resolution or improvement graph. Passing code/tests
provide evidence for review and keep conclusions revisable.
