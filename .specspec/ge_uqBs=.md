# Open the agent guide with what the graph is for

The guide begins with two paragraphs of purpose, ahead of every rule. The first
expands the name specification spectrum and describes the graph: a root that states
what the product is for, every other node a conclusion drawn from its parents, which
are its intentions, and nodes that run from what the owner wants through ever more
concrete conclusions to the files.

The second paragraph gives the reason for the graph. A specification worked out with
a coding agent blends the outcomes the owner wants with choices that were proposed
and accepted along the way. Separate nodes keep a choice replaceable while its
outcome stays, and let the owner specify one branch in detail and delegate another.
The paragraph ends with the agent's part: recording each of its own choices as a node
under the intention it serves.

Knowing the purpose lets an agent apply the rules to situations they leave open.
