# Write node sentences that stand on what the product does

A node sentence says what the product does and is complete for a reader who knows
nothing else. Three kinds of contrast lean on something outside it: an earlier version
of the product, the way the owner worked before the product, and something the product
is not. In each case the agent writes the statement itself. What the owner says about
today's way of working is the reason for an intention and belongs in the conversation and
in the commit message. A boundary the product keeps is written as what the product
does, as in "patient data stays in the EU".
