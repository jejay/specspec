# Set the hovered card and its direct neighbors apart from the rest of the graph

While the owner hovers a card, that card and the cards directly linked to it are readable as they are, and every other card and every link that does not touch the hovered card is darkened. When the pointer leaves the card, the graph returns to its ordinary appearance. This works in the overview, in the view of a selected node and with an expanded card, in both graph editors. The direction of the links and the Git marks are readable during the hover, and the darkening is presentation that lasts as long as the hover.
