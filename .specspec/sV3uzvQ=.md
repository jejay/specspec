# Show the zoomed-out graph as solid colored node shapes

As the camera zooms out, the backgrounds of nodes grow stronger step by step until, at the far limit, they match their visible borders. This goes together with the quieting of ordinary arrows. At that distance the visual structure and the meaning of the colors take priority over headlines and the other text of the cards. Shapes, Git marks, spotlight highlights, forms, geometry and the layout of the world are the same at every zoom level, in both graph editors.
