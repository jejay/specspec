# Tell the owner's intentions from their conclusions before anything else

With whatever the owner says or writes, the agent first works out what the owner intends and which conclusions the owner has already drawn from it. The wording rarely says which is which. A programming language is part of the intention of a Python library and belongs in its manifesto, while for an app whose owner has no preference it is a conclusion far down that the agent draws. The agent infers what the owner holds as intention from what they say, from the reasons they give and from the product. It states its reading, and it asks where it cannot tell and a wrong reading would cost much.

This comes before every other rule about structure. An agent that knows which statement is the intention and which the conclusion is trusted to place and word the nodes sensibly, also in cases the rules leave open.
