# Follow the guide's opening with a short working routine

The packaged AGENTS.md guide stays within about 10 KB. Right after its opening
statement of purpose comes a numbered routine: check owner changes and spotlights,
read relevant nodes with their ancestors in bounded queries, work in the requested
phase, edit through add, update and delete, check the graph and its source links
after changes, validate the exact index before a commit, and report
choices and uncertainty. Concepts and phase rules follow, then the editing, deletion,
source-link and recovery commands, all visible in help, with `--help` as the
reference.
