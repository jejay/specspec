# Measure first whether an agent tells intention from conclusion

The quizzes look first at two things: whether the agent infers what the owner holds as intention, and whether it keeps an intention and the conclusion drawn from it apart, in a drawn graph and in a discussion. They include an owner's statement that makes no sense for the manifesto's product, a statement whose firmness the wording leaves unclear, and a manifesto with a conclusion in its wording. The scorecard shows these behaviours first. The other behaviours keep their quizzes and weigh less: an agent that knows what is intention and what is conclusion is trusted with the edge cases.
