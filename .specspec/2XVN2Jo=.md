# Keep lines and arrowheads at a visible size on screen at every zoom level

A line is at least 1.6 CSS pixels wide on screen and an arrowhead at least 6 pixels long, whatever the zoom level. At higher magnification they have their ordinary sizes of 1.7 and 10 world units. The dashes of the Git line patterns are lengthened in the same way when the graph is zoomed out. A port has a clearance of 3 world units from the outline of its card. The ports of an ordinary card are spread over its full height, and the ports of the round manifesto lie on the chords of its circle.

A link leaves its card in a short straight segment and approaches its arrowhead along a horizontal stretch of 64 world units, which is long enough for the arrowhead at the lowest supported zoom. The line ends at the base of the filled arrowhead, so that curve and triangle join cleanly. The corridors around the cards in the way and the loops of links that point against the reading direction, with their direction, are the same at every zoom level. A camera change updates the appearance of the drawn links, and the positions in the world, the forms and the data of the nodes stay as they are.
