# Emphasize a hovered color in its own hue, on card borders and on links

On hover the border of a colored card is emphasized more strongly in its own hue, on compact and on expanded cards, in every view of both graph editors. The neutral manifesto of the distance and type modes has a white border on hover, and a manifesto that is colored by revision status has a border in the hue of its status.

A colored curve and its arrowhead that touch the hovered card gain both brightness and saturation in their own hue. The emphasis goes together with the darkening outside the direct neighborhood. The line patterns of the links, the pairing of each curve with its arrowhead, the direction of the links, the return to the ordinary paint after the hover, the form elements and the geometry are the same with and without the emphasis.
