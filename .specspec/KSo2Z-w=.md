# Guide versions kept as files

`guides.py` resolves every name to a file under `tools/guide_quiz/guides/`. The name
`shipped` stands for the package template and resolves to the dated file with the same
content, so that every result names a text that stays; without such a file the tool asks
for a copy. Six files are dated states of the shipped guide: the one released with
0.0.1, the one of 2026-11-10 before the structure rules, the one of 2026-11-11 with them,
the one of 2026-11-11 with the structure review and the fuller contrast rule, the one of 2026-11-11 with the seal of agent planning commits, and the one of 2026-11-11 that teaches intention and conclusion as one relation, 14.3 KB. Seven
files under `guides/experiments/` are variants of the 2026-11-10 guide: five lines only,
worked examples only, rule sentences, rule sentences with a phase sentence or with a
check sentence, rule sentences with worked examples, and a long rules section. Fourteen
more are variants of the guide with the structure review: four trimmed versions of 11.5,
9.9, 7.1 and 3.6 KB, each cutting further than the one before; the second and the third
of them with the sentences about depth put back; a candidate of 8.2 KB that keeps what
the quizzes showed a loss without; four copies of the candidate with other wordings of
tandem planning; the guide with only its Commands section shortened, and with only its
tandem planning paragraph replaced; and one whose
structure review also names merging. Eight more lead to the guide with the seal: two drafts, the second with its Commands section shortened and with its asides removed as well; a third draft with the full and with the short Commands section; and two texts with the sentence on breadth and depth, one in the opening and one at the end of tandem planning. Eight more lead to the guide with one relation. Three are a first draft: alone, with a sentence that checks every link by asking why, and with a rule to draw from the root outward. Three are copies of the shipped text without one passage each: the paragraph on telling intention from conclusion first, the paragraph on holding conclusions against intentions, and the two questions for placing a statement. One calls a list of limits one decision, asks for a node per own conclusion in the summary and shortens the paragraph on holding conclusions against intentions, and one says where the agent plans what depth consists of.
