# Preview the repository with its headline, a graph pictogram and its license

The repository's social preview in its GitHub settings is a 1280 × 640 card on the dark brand surface. The dark logo stands at the top left above the headline The Missing Why in Your Specification. Why is set in italics, slanted by 10° from Manrope's upright letters, in the violet of the spectrum's why end.

Beside the headline, a pictogram explains the concept with a graph of shapes and colors alone: a ring for the manifesto, rounded cards with two lines for requirements and squares for implementation, colored by depth along the brand spectrum, above a bar that runs from why to how. Heavier spectrum lines trace one implementation node through all its ancestors to the manifesto, across a node with two parents. All nodes are colored, so the other branches read as parts of the same specification.

A line at the bottom shows the GitHub mark with the repository name jejay/specspec and MIT License.
