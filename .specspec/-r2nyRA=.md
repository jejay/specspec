# Show in the guide how an owner's sentence becomes an outcome and a choice

After its passage on what belongs in a node, the guide shows three sentences an owner
might say, each followed by the outcome and the choice it becomes, in the indented
format of the example graph. The sentences are "a timeline, so everyone sees when
things happen", "an SMS when the parcel ships" and "the open tickets on the office
TV". The first gives its purpose. The other two name only a way, and the agent words
the outcome above it. All three ways are things people see. Worked examples teach this
rule: stated as a principle, agents apply it to technology only.
