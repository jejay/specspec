# Show in the guide how an owner's sentence becomes an intention and a conclusion

After its passage on where a statement goes, the guide shows three sentences an owner might say, each followed by the intention and the conclusion it becomes, in the indented format of the example graph. The sentences are "a timeline, so everyone sees when things happen", "an SMS when the parcel ships" and "the open tickets on the office TV". The first gives its intention. The other two state only a way, and the agent words the intention above it. All three ways are things people see. Worked examples teach this rule: stated as a principle, agents apply it to technology only.
