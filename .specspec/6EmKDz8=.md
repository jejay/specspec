# Read the graph from left to right, from intentions to conclusions

The owner reads from the picture what was concluded from what. In the overview the manifesto stands at the left. Every node stands to the right of its parents and to the left of its children, and every relationship is drawn as an arrow that points from the intention to the conclusion.

A node's place between the manifesto and the files follows from its links alone, and the picture shows that place as a gradient from left to right. Intention and conclusion are the two ends of each arrow: every node except the manifesto is a conclusion from the parents on its left and an intention for the children on its right.

The view of a selected node reads the same way as the overview: the direct parents of the selected node stand on its left and its direct children on its right.
