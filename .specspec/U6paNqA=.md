# Explain specspec plainly, from the problem it answers to the limits of the current release

The introduction to specspec presents the problem and the idea before the details of setup. The problem is that the assumptions of a coding agent pile up in a specification, and the idea is that every solution stays separately revisable. The introduction explains how the specification spectrum connects intentions, conclusions and implementation, and how an owner chooses how much to direct and how much to delegate. It uses the selected branding and an example that is easy to understand.

The introduction persuades through clarity: it explains the core idea plainly, specifically and candidly, as an engineer would describe their own tool. It says concretely what the current release can do and where its limits are, and it claims nothing beyond that. Whether a product is correct is for the owner to judge, the owner reviews as much as they choose, and the owner starts and directs their own coding agent.
