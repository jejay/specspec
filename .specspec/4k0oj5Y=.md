# Teach where a statement goes with two questions

The guide's passage on what belongs in a node asks two questions about a statement. What is it for: the answer is its intention and goes above it, and the agent words that intention as a node when the graph lacks it, in words that name no way of achieving it. What follows from it that building needs and nobody has said: those conclusions are the agent's to draw, each in a node of its own beneath the statement. The first question makes an agent put an intention above a way the owner states. The second keeps it drawing the conclusions that the owner left to it, which a guide that only asks upward crowds out.
