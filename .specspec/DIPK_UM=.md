# Show each kind of contrast with an example in the guide

The guide's writing rules give a short example for each kind of contrast, next to the
plain statement that replaces it: an earlier version, the owner's way of working before
the product, and what the product is not. The examples come from the planning app of the example graph. The owner's way of working before the product has two, one of them a sentence that begins with nobody: an intention that the agent reads from today's trouble is easily worded as what no longer happens.
