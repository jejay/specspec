# Teach the guide's concepts with one vocabulary and one small example graph

The guide names the relation between nodes with one pair of words, intention and conclusion, and uses them for the two ends of a link: a node is a conclusion from its parents and an intention for its children. Kinds of node have no words of their own beyond the node types, which appear under their own names: manifesto, requirement and implementation. The word choice appears at most where the guide characterizes what a conclusion is, and stands for conclusion nowhere. A four-line example graph from the README's planning app shows one node of each type. The text after it replaces a conclusion beneath its unchanged intention, and then that intention beneath the unchanged manifesto. The writing rules show both kinds of historic contrasting on one example sentence. Every phase section says what starts the phase.
