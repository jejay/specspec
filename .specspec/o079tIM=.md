# Teach the guide's concepts with one vocabulary and one small example graph

The guide names what nodes say with two words, outcome and choice, and the two sides
of a link with two more, intention and conclusion. Node types appear under their own
names: manifesto, requirement and implementation. A four-line example graph from the
README's planning app shows one node of each type around an outcome and a choice, and
the text after it replaces the choice beneath its unchanged outcome. The writing
rules show both kinds of historic contrasting on one example sentence. Every phase
section says what starts the phase.
