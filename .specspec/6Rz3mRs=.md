# Review the structure of the owner's own edits before planning

The agent holds what the owner changed by hand since the newest agent planning commit against the rules for structure and wording: one statement per node, an intention above the conclusion drawn from it, the parents a node names, what gets a node at all and how node text is written. It tells the owner what it would split, merge, move or reword and why, keeps to points that matter for the graph, and leaves the graph as it is.

Only a suggestion the owner accepts is carried out, in a commit of the agent's planning. Every other edit stays in the owner's words.

The phase is called structure review and lies between owner planning and tandem planning. An owner who works through conversation alone never meets it.
