# Review the structure of the owner's own edits before planning

When the owner has edited nodes by hand, the agent first holds those edits against the
rules for structure and wording: one decision per node, an outcome above the way that
serves it, the parents a node names, what gets a node at all and how node text is
written. It tells the owner, node by node, what it would split, move or reword and why,
and it leaves the graph as it is.

Only a suggestion the owner accepts is carried out. It becomes part of the agent's own
changes in agent planning, after the owner's edits are committed as the owner wrote
them. Every other edit stays in the owner's words.

The phase is called structure review and lies between owner planning and tandem
planning. An owner who works through conversation alone never meets it.
