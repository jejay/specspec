# Lead the guide's tandem paragraph with telling intentions from conclusions

The guide's paragraph on tandem planning gives its instructions in this order: discuss and leave the graph as it is; tell the owner's intentions from the conclusions they have already drawn and say how each was read; hold each conclusion against its intention, object where one does not fit and propose own ones; mention removals, splits and changes of existing nodes at once; close with guiding questions and a tentative answer to each. Telling intentions from conclusions comes first among the things to do, because a lightweight model follows the leading instruction of a paragraph most reliably.
