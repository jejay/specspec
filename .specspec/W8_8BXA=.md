# Derive the place of a node on the spectrum from its links

The place of a node between the manifesto and the files follows from its links to intentions and conclusions. Refining a node adds requirements beneath it, and realizing it adds implementation leaves. The distance of a node from the manifesto and what lies beneath it are derived from the graph of the selected revision.
