# Use a neutral gray manifesto in distance and type modes

Use a light-gray manifesto accent in compact and expanded distance/type views.
Share the ordinary theme text and modest card-tint treatment, with matching gray
legend entries. Its neutral hover border is white. Git/Revision Status mode uses
its comparison-derived fill and hue-emphasizing border like other cards.
