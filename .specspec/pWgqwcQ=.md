# Use a neutral gray manifesto in distance and type modes

In the distance and type modes the manifesto has a light-gray accent, as a compact card and as an expanded one. It has the ordinary text color of the theme and the modest tint of the other cards, and the legend shows matching gray entries. On hover its neutral border is white. In Git/Revision Status mode the manifesto takes its fill from the comparison and has the border that emphasizes its hue, like the other cards.
