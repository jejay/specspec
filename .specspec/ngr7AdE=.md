# State in the guide what stays one node and what stays out of the graph

The guide's passage on what belongs in a node names a list of limits as its example of
one decision that stays together. One further sentence names what stays out of the
graph: an order of work, an option that was not chosen, a topic that only groups other
nodes and a rewording of the parent. A short statement is enough for each of these
rules, because agents apply them as soon as they read them.
