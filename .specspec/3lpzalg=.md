# Keep values that one decision settles together in one node

Limits, fields or cases that one decision settles stay in one node, as a list. A value
is edited in place, while an approach is replaced. An item becomes a node of its own
when it has a reason of its own, decisions beneath it or a different approach that
could take its place.
