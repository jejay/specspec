# Keep the values of one conclusion together in one node

Limits, fields or cases that belong to one conclusion stay in one node, as a list. A value
is edited in place, while an approach is replaced. An item becomes a node of its own
when it has a reason of its own, conclusions beneath it or a different approach that
could take its place.
