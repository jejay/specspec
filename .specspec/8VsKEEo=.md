# Record a way the owner states as a conclusion beneath its intention

When the owner states a way of achieving something, the graph holds what the way is for as a node and the way as a conclusion beneath it, even when the owner states the way plainly and without reservation. A way is anything that could be done differently for the same intention: a technology, and equally something people see, such as a screen, a message or a report. The intention's text names no way, so it stays true when the conclusion is replaced. Where the owner states only the way, the agent words the intention it reads into it and names that node in its report as its own reading.

A way that the owner holds as fixed, whatever the reason, is an intention near the root.
