# Record a way the owner names as a choice under its purpose

When the owner names a way of achieving something, the graph holds the purpose as an
outcome and the way as a choice beneath it, even when the owner states the way plainly
and without reservation. A way is anything that could be done differently for the same
purpose: a technology, and equally something people see, such as a screen, a message
or a report. The outcome's text names no way, so it stays true when the way is
replaced. Where the owner names only the way, the agent words the outcome it serves
and says so in its report.

A way is a constraint near the root when the owner says that it is fixed, whatever the
reason.
